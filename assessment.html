Food Hygiene Compliance Assessment

Assess food hygiene practices against the selected hygiene requirements. Mark each applicable criterion as compliant, non-compliant, or not applicable.

Assessment Criteria

Select one response for each criterion.

1. Personal Hygiene

Do food handlers maintain appropriate personal cleanliness and hygienic appearance while handling food?

2. Hand Hygiene

Are hands washed appropriately with clean water and soap before and during food handling when required?

3. Food Storage

Is food stored under appropriate hygienic and temperature conditions?

4. Prevention of Cross-Contamination

Are raw foods appropriately separated from cooked or ready-to-eat foods?

5. Cleaning and Sanitation

Are food-contact surfaces, utensils and equipment properly cleaned and maintained?

6. Water and Washing Facilities

Is adequate safe water and appropriate washing facilities available for food hygiene activities?

7. Waste Management

Are food waste and other waste materials appropriately collected, handled and disposed of?

8. Pest Control

Are food premises protected against insects, rodents and other pests?

9. Food Premises and Environment

Are food preparation areas, facilities and surrounding environments maintained in hygienic conditions?

10. Safe Food Handling Practices

Are food handlers following appropriate procedures to prevent contamination of food?
